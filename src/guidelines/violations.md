# Violations

## Goal

A lot of times, we need to violate the guidelines, rules or our own dignity.
Sometimes it's because a library doesn't support our use case yet, or because
we are on a tight schedule and can't spend time on given task.

In such cases, it's really important to document the violation. A simple
comment describing the violation is enough.

## Why

Any good systems will have a lot of violations. It's important to document
them, so that in the future somebody can correct them - or at least have a
proper context for reasoning behind them.

## Instructions

This gives a great rule of thumb for comments - they should be present if you
are straying away from the guidelines, violating them. When things are
predictable, it's easier to read just the code than to read the comment and
the code. That's why we should minimize the comments, so they stand out when
they are needed.
